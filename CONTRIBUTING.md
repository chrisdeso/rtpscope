# Contributing

## Working agreement
- Chat replies within 24 h on weekdays, 48 h on weekends.
- Async standups in Discord (bot-prompted); a weekly 20-minute sync.
- `main` is always demoable and protected: changes need one approving review and passing CI.
- Branches `<area>/<short-desc>`, Conventional Commits, draft PRs early, `Closes #N`. See the README.
- Log your work in `CONTRIBUTIONS.md` — the final report requires an exact division of labor.
- Escalation: two missed commitments lead to a re-scoping call; if still unresolved a week later, we send a joint email to the instructor.
